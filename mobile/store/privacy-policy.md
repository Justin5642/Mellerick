Mellerick Field is a business tool used by field-service teams whose accounts are
issued by their employer ("your organisation"). This policy explains what the app
collects, why, and your choices.

## Information we collect
- **Account details** — the email address you sign in with, and your name and role
  as set up by your organisation.
- **Location** — if you grant permission, your device location is used to geo-tag
  site photos and to clock you in/out and record travel time when you arrive at or
  leave a job site. You can decline or revoke this at any time in your device
  settings; the app still works, without those automatic features.
- **Photos** — photos you capture or attach for jobs, evidence and receipts.
- **Voice recordings** — job-completion voice reports you choose to record; these are
  transcribed to text via a transcription service (`<TRANSCRIPTION_PROVIDER>`).
- **Job and business records** — the jobs, customers, quotes, invoices, timesheets,
  variations and equipment data you and your organisation enter to run the service.

We do **not** use any advertising or cross-app tracking, and we do **not** include
third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

## How we use it
Solely to provide the field-service functionality: authenticating you, showing your
jobs, recording time, storing photos/signatures/reports, and syncing your work to
your organisation's account. Field data is stored on your device so the app works
offline, and syncs to your organisation's account when you reconnect.

## How it's stored and shared
- Data is stored in our backend (Supabase — Postgres, Storage and Authentication) in
  the `<REGION, e.g. Australia>` region, and transmitted over encrypted HTTPS/TLS.
- We share data with service providers only to run the app: `<TRANSCRIPTION_PROVIDER>`
  (voice-report transcription), our email provider (`<EMAIL_PROVIDER, e.g. Resend>`,
  to send invoices/quotes your organisation issues), and, if your organisation
  connects it, your accounting system (`<XERO/…>`) for invoices and expenses.
- We do **not** sell your data or share it for advertising.
