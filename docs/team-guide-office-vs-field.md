# Mellerick Office and Mellerick Field — which one do I use?

There are two Mellerick apps. They share the same jobs and data.

| | **Mellerick Office** | **Mellerick Field** |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | The website | The phone app |
| Where | Laptop / office PC | Your phone |
| Looks like | Dark **OFFICE** tag beside the logo | Phone app icon, installed from TestFlight / the app store |
| Address | https://mellerick-app.vercel.app | — |
| Who | Office and admin | Technicians, drivers (office too, for the schedule on the go) |

**Use Mellerick Office for:** the schedule board, POs and supplier invoices,
invoicing and Xero, reports and job margins, fleet costs and hire rates,
staff and leave, settings, approvals.

**Use Mellerick Field for:** my jobs, clocking on and off (including
automatic clocking), photos, notes, sign-off, Day Works and truck loads,
supplier orders, logging mistakes, "Didn't make it". It works with no signal.

Rule of thumb: **at a desk → Office. On site → Field.**

## Put Mellerick Office on your laptop (one time)

1. Open **Chrome** or **Edge** and go to https://mellerick-app.vercel.app.
2. Sign in.
3. **Chrome:** click the install icon at the right of the address bar
   (a screen with a down arrow) → **Install**. Or ⋮ menu → **Cast, save and
   share** → **Install page as app**.
   **Edge:** ⋯ menu → **Apps** → **Install this site as an app**.
4. It opens in its own window called **Mellerick Office**, with the OFFICE
   icon. Right-click its icon in the taskbar → **Pin to taskbar**.

On a Mac with Safari: **File → Add to Dock**.

Don't install the website on your phone — use the Mellerick Field app there.

## Signing in

Same email and password on both. Forgot it? Use **Forgot password?** on the
sign-in screen of either app.
